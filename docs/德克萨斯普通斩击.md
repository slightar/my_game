# 德克萨斯普通形态半圆斩击

参考资料：
- https://prts.wiki/w/德克萨斯/spine ：默认战斗正面模型标识 char_102_texas，与项目 texas_battle.json 一致。
- https://prts.wiki/w/德克萨斯 ：普通德克萨斯与技能资料。

当前版本为根据半圆刀光要求制作的视觉近似，不是从上述网页提取的原版攻击特效。原来的普通攻击复用了双重交叉斩图集，现已拆分。

资源：assets/operators/texas_normal_slash.png，4 列 × 3 行、12 帧、透明 RGBA。使用内置 imagegen 生成；不使用长方形、三角形或运行时线段拼刀光。逐帧起刀、展开、消散，固定支点，向左时镜像。普通近战播放 0.17 秒；远程剑气展开后保持半圆，结束时消散。伤害、判定、冷却和命中／击杀震动沿用现有设置。

验证：character_render_smoke 检查资源加载、左右前方像素覆盖、动画到期消失，以及普通攻击完整动作和命中窗口。预览在 cmake-build-debug/character-qa/texas-normal-crescent.png。

## 生成提示词（内置 imagegen）

Create a production-ready 2D game VFX SPRITE SHEET for a normal sword swing, anime tactical game aesthetic similar to Arknights Texas normal melee. This is ONLY a SINGLE FORWARD SEMICIRCULAR CRESCENT sword trail, never a complete circle, never crossing blades or a spiral. Transparent RGBA background. Exact regular grid: 4 columns x 3 rows, 12 equally sized cells, row-major animation order. Square cells, sheet aspect 4:3. No text, no numbers, no borders, no checkerboard, no weapons or characters. Every frame has exactly the SAME fixed pivot at cell center and same maximum radius 35% of cell width, leave 12% margin on all sides. Crescent lives on the RIGHT HALF of the circle, opening faces LEFT; endpoints near 12 o'clock and 6 o'clock. White bright cutting rim, subtle cool pale grey-blue inner falloff, a fine desaturated warm cream edge; no red, no saturated blue. Beautiful hand-painted textured brushstroke taper, sharp tips and subtle fragmenting wisps, no straight polygons. Show clear coherent chronological animation: frames 1-2 thin short arc ignites at upper right; 3-4 arc sweeps clockwise and grows; frames 5-7 form ONE pronounced approximately 180-degree right-facing crescent, thick bright middle at right, top/bottom ends taper fine; frames 8-10 progressively taper and break up trailing paint from the top toward bottom right; frames 11-12 sparse fading wisps with low opacity. Peak thickness 12% of radius. All frames are unique chronological shapes, not merely rotated copies, NOT a contact sheet of unrelated variants. Center all frames consistently; transparent negative space inside and outside the crescent. It should read as one crisp half-moon blade slash in front of a swordswoman.
